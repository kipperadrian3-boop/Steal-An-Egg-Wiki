Egg Mutations & Multipliers

Mutations multiply the base income of a hatched pet. They stack with size multipliers.

Mutation Income Multiplier How to Obtain
Silver 1.25x Random hatch chance from any nest
Bloom 1.5x Sakura Incubator in the Cherry Blossom biome
Golden 2.0x Random hatch chance or Golden Egg nests
Rainbow 2.5x Rare world mutation roll
Spirit Bloom 3.0x Sakura Incubator roll (Cherry Blossom endgame)
🌸 Sakura Incubator: Unlocked in the Cherry Blossom biome by sacrificing a Crane pet. Allows applying Bloom and Spirit Bloom mutations.
