⚡ Core Gameplay Loop

Master these fundamentals to progress from a forest scavenger to an endgame powerhouse.

1. Steal Eggs from Nests

Phase 1
Venture out into the biomes. Locate nests holding eggs. The larger and more exotic the egg, the higher the rarity and multiplier inside! Beware: touching an egg immediately triggers the biome guardian.
Risk Level: High Reset: ~5 Minutes

2. Escape the Guardian

Phase 2
Once you pick up an egg, the parent beast will hunt you down. You must carry the egg back safely to your base pen. If you get hit, you lose the egg and get flung back!
Crucial Stat: Speed Tool: Bat / Evasion

3. Hatch & Generate Passive Cash

Phase 3
Drop the egg into your Pen incubator to hatch it. Each pet produces passive cash per second ($/s). Mutations (Golden, Rainbow, Spirit Bloom) multiply this income up to 3x!
Income: Passive $/s Rarities: Common to Divine

4. Upgrade Base & Treadmill

Phase 4
Reinvest your pet earnings into the Treadmill to pump your movement speed, and expand your Pen capacity to house more simultaneous pets. Speed unlocks access to higher tier biomes.
Upgrades: Pen & Speed Goal: Cherry Blossom

Mutations apply multiplicative multipliers to your pets' base income. Mutations stack with Egg Size bonuses.

🌸 The Sakura Incubator

Cherry Blossom Zone
Located deep inside the Cherry Blossom biome. Unlocking the incubator requires obtaining the Sakura Crane pet from the branch nests. Once unlocked, you can place eggs inside to roll exclusive Bloom (1.5x) and Spirit Bloom (3.0x) mutations.
Unlock: Sakura Crane Max Multiplier: 3.0x

Formula

🏏 Weapons, Base Pen & Treadmill Scaling

Speed is power, and bats are your defensive shield. Upgrade your tools to survive PvP and guardian attacks.

Wooden to Cosmic Bats

PvP Weapon
Equip bats to knock out competing players holding eggs and snatch their loot. Bats are also used during interactive world events like the Great Bloom to smash enchanted trees for rare mutation seeds.
Knockback: Upgradable Range: Melee Arc

Base Pen Expansion

Capacity
Your initial pen only holds a small number of pets. Upgrading pen capacity increases the number of pets generating income simultaneously. Essential for compounding your hourly cashflow.
Max Slots: 25+ Compounding: High

Treadmill Speed Training

Speed Generator
Train on your base treadmill while AFK or between runs. Higher-tier treadmills provide accelerated speed gains per second, allowing you to bridge the gap into late-game zones.
AFK Friendly: Yes Max Cap: 2.5B+
